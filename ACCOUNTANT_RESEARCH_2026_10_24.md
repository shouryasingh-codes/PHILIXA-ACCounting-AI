# Accountant workflow research and test rationale

Ten frozen synthetic scenarios (nine unique invoices) were tested after this research. First run:8/10. The clean PNG and mildly tilted JPEG failed because wrapped GST/serial headings were parsed as incorrect columns. The parser now distinguishes explicit header fragments without ignoring incomplete actual rows, retaining original evidence coordinates. Frozen documents and expected fields were not changed. Full local re-extraction then passed10/10, including original wrong-total preservation/rejection, company/page blocks and hash/business-identity duplicate detection. This is development validation, not held-out real accuracy. No cloud calls or native Tally posting occurred.

45 regression tests passed, including persistent source/duplicate guards and an incomplete actual photo-row rejection. Existing Day9 baseline also passed:166/166 extraction fields,7/7 mappings,8/8 accounting and4/4 simulated posting cases. Test teardown still emits existing open-connection ResourceWarnings. Reports and first/final run archives: data/accountant-pack/. Reproduce with `python -m scripts.evaluate_accountant_pack` inside the app container. Test inputs: scripts/fixtures/accountant-v1/; printable/download pack: output/pdf/accountant-v1/accountant-10-scenarios.zip. Browser upload/reload and new native Tally posting were not exercised in this round. Photos remain reviewed operation. The idle document worker was restarted to load the fix.

Research date:24 October2026. Desk research, not an interview or observation of an accountant. This is a purchase-entry assistant evaluation, not a claim to replace an accountant. Salary alone does not define duties or expertise. No measured percentage of accountant time is assumed.

## What the job actually includes

An employer-authored Accounts Executive description includes Tally entries and supporting voucher files, petty cash, purchase/sales/receipt entries, bank and ledger reconciliation, statutory working papers and month-end reporting. That establishes a much broader job than typing a purchase voucher. Source: [Villgro employer JD](https://villgro.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Accounts-Executive-JD-2024.docx.pdf). It is a nonprofit role, so donor/payroll duties are not assumed to represent every trading business. A sampled junior-accountant listing at INR15,000–20,000/month includes daily Tally work, AP/AR, BRS and GST/TDS assistance; it is an example, not a representative salary survey: [listing](https://in.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=c0d674ffea7ae676).

For the initial Philixa use case, the working persona is an accounts assistant in a goods-trading business who receives supplier bills, records supported purchases and resolves exceptions with the owner/senior accountant. CA-office multi-client work, manufacturing, payroll, services, pharma batch/expiry and statutory filing need separate specifications.

## A purchase from document to checked books

1. Intake: collect the supplier invoice and keep its original. Check who the buyer is, who issued it, its reference/date, completeness and whether it was already recorded. Photos, resends and multiple pages make this a source-control problem as well as a reading problem. Rule46 specifies identifiers, dates, descriptions, quantities/units and amounts: [CBIC rules](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001). Required details depend on transaction circumstances; this research does not certify a legal tax invoice.
2. Commercial check: compare quantities, agreed prices, discounts and receipt evidence where the business uses it. Tally supports purchase orders, receipt notes and purchase links, but the sequence is optional and business-specific. An invoice alone does not prove that goods arrived. [Official purchase-order/receipt-note workflow](https://help.tallysolutions.com/purchase-order-tally/).
3. Record: select the correct company/registration, preserve supplier invoice reference/date, select the actual party, purchase account and items, enter quantity/rate, then apply configured tax ledgers. Philixa must not invent identities from a fuzzy spelling match. [Official GST purchase workflow](https://help.tallysolutions.com/gst-purchases-tally/).
4. Exceptions: a mismatch is investigated rather than silently balanced. Wrong company, missing pages, unclear item identity and unsupported tax treatments need review/blocking; a reuploaded file must not create another purchase. This is our engineering acceptance inference from the source and accounting workflow, not a quoted universal office procedure.
5. After saving: verify the actual voucher, stock effect and amount owed. Credit purchases continue into invoice-level dues/payment matching; returns, receipt-note tracking and landed costs are additional workflows. [Official purchase lifecycle](https://help.tallysolutions.com/purchase-process-in-tallyprime/).
6. Settlement/month end: bill-wise tracking links receipts/payments to outstanding references. A total party balance is not invoice-level matching. [Tracking methods](https://help.tallysolutions.com/receivables-payables-tracking-methods/). Bank reconciliation compares books with actual bank activity; Tally already supports statement import and exact/potential matching, so any Philixa bank feature must beat an appropriate existing baseline. [Official bank reconciliation](https://help.tallysolutions.com/bank-reconciliation-tally/).

GST preparation is also separate from calculating GST on one invoice. GSTN advises reconciliation of GSTR2B with books and avoiding duplicate credit; invoice extraction is not an ITC entitlement or filing decision. [GSTN GSTR2B FAQ](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQ_gstr2b.htm). No portal filing, eligibility decision or payment is part of this test.

## Implications for this test pack

Freeze the expected original fields before extraction. Score reading separately from accounting/identity guards, and disclose which guard is exercised. Six supported-source cases cover clean local purchase, multiple items/mixed rates with discount, rounding, interstate purchase, PNG and a mildly tilted JPEG. Four exception cases cover a wrong printed total, wrong buyer, incomplete pagination and a renamed duplicate. Ten scenarios contain nine unique invoices; the duplicate is intentionally the same original. Supplier GSTINs, addresses and goods/rates are dummy fixture data; tax rates come from synthetic configured masters, not commodity-rate advice.

Positive cases should become reviewable correct drafts; photos are not auto-post candidates. Negative cases should preserve the original discrepancy and block unsafe continuation or identify the existing document. No automatic posting, native master edits or new Tally vouchers are needed for this round.

This pack cannot establish representative real-world accuracy, blur/handwriting coverage, accountant correction time, cash/credit/payment completion, PO/GRN matching, bank reconciliation, GST filing or the50% handling-time target. Failures and first-run reports must remain visible; fixing a case makes it development evidence rather than untouched validation.
